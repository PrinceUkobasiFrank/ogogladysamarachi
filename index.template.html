About

I'm Gladys Ogo, known as The Keen-Eyed Proofreader — a law graduate and legal proofreader. Beyond legal proofreading, I lead conversations at the intersection of law, digital visibility and career development, working with legal brands, career organisations and communities while building a growing presence among law students and young legal professionals.

Through my content, speaking, consultations, brand collaborations and the Premium Law Students Community (PLSC), I turn these conversations into practical learning experiences around professional visibility in the evolving legal profession.

Premium Law Students Community

Building law students who stand out among others.

Studying law can make you feel like you have to choose between your academics and everything else — build your brand later, network later, think about your career later, just read your books. PLSC was built around a different idea: you can take your academics seriously and still begin preparing for the professional world.

Who it's for

Law students who are intentional about academic excellence, professional growth, digital visibility and meaningful opportunities.

How it works

Through training, mentorship, accountability, challenges and practical sessions, PLSC lets you start developing before graduation rather than waiting until you're suddenly expected to know what to do.
